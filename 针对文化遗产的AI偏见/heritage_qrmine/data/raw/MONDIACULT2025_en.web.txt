L15@P0: 1. Shared Vision
L16@P0: We reaffirm the recognition of the right to take part in cultural life as a human right, inherent to dignity and identity.
L17@P0: Culture contributes to the full exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms, peacebuilding, inclusive economic
L18@P0: growth, climate resilience, wellbeing and sustainable development. It is a global public good, with intrinsic value,
L19@P0: whose transformative power must be fully mobilized to address today’s challenges and to shape more just, equitable,
L20@P0: peaceful, intercultural, inclusive and sustainable societies.
L21@P0: 2. Strategic Foundations
L22@P0: This document builds on an inclusive, multi-stakeholder process, including regional consultations, global surveys, and
L23@P0: contributions from multiple stakeholders, as well as reflections arising from UNESCO’s six cultural conventions.
L24@P0: Grounded in the six priority domains of MONDIACULT 2022 Declaration and with an ethical, human rights-based and
L25@P0: human-centred approach, itincorporates two focus areas from the preparatory process: culture for peace, and artificial
L26@P0: intelligence (AI) and culture. These pillars provide a common foundation for collective action in favour of integrating
L27@P0: culture in the post-2030 United Nations (UN) development framework.

L31@P0: priority for cultural policies and practices at all levels.

L65@P1: 3. Cultural Economy and Decent Work
L66@P1: • Recognizing the cultural and creative sectors and industries as a fundamental driver of inclusive and
L67@P1: sustainable economic development, enabling innovation, and contributing a significant share to national,
L68@P1: regional, and global economies.

L79@P1: imbalances in the circulation of cultural goods and services, and the unequal distribution and visibility of
